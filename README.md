# Rang Rivaaj

A clothing store website I made as a front-end project. It sells kurtas, t-shirts, hoodies and dupattas for men, women and kids, and works on both phone and desktop.

Live site: https://waris-codes.github.io/rang-rivaaj-store/

## What it does

- Browse 12 products and filter by category or colour
- Search and sort by price
- Product pages with size selection, size guide and related items
- Cart with quantities, delivery charge and a free delivery limit (Rs 5,000)
- Wishlist, and the cart and wishlist are saved when you refresh the page
- Checkout form that checks the name, Pakistani mobile number, city and address
- Contact form and newsletter signup
- Light and dark mode, based on your device settings

## Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks or libraries. Fonts come from Google Fonts.

## Files

- `index.html` - page layout
- `style.css` - styling
- `script.js` - products, pages, cart, wishlist and forms
- `images/` - product photos (named 1.jpg to 12.jpg, matching the product ids in `script.js`)

## Running it

Download the folder and open `index.html` in a browser. There is nothing to install.

## Notes

- This is a front-end project only. There is no database or server, so orders and messages are not saved anywhere.
- Product names, prices and reviews are sample data.
- The product images are AI-generated.
